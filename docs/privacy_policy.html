PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated September 01, 2026

This Privacy Notice for Francisco Javier Bonilla Varo (doing business as BoniFit) ("we", "us", or "our"), describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you:

  • Visit our website at https://bonifit.site or any website of ours that links to this Privacy Notice
  • Download and use our mobile application (BoniFit), or any other application of ours that links to this Privacy Notice
  • Use BoniFit. BoniFit is an Android fitness application that lets users create and manage workout routines, browse an exercise catalogue, track physical progress (weight, body fat percentage, BMI and mood), monitor their training activity and personal records, and access healthy recipes and supplement guides. It includes BoniCoach, an AI-powered conversational assistant specialised in training and nutrition. The application is free to use and supported by advertising.
  • Engage with us in other related ways, including any marketing or events

Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. We are responsible for making decisions about how your personal information is processed. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at fbonillavaro@gmail.com.
